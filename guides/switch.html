Nintendo Switch

The Switch can move an installed game to its microSD card. The card's Nintendo folder then holds that version, and you can keep a copy of that folder on a computer. It works only with the same Switch.

On this page
  1. What you need
  2. Stop updates
  3. Move Fortnite to the microSD card
  4. Copy the card to a computer
  5. Put it back
  6. Common problems

1. What you need

  • A microSD card (UHS-I is fastest; 64 GB or more for Fortnite). Cards over 32 GB use exFAT, which the Switch may need a free system update to read; it offers it when you insert the card.
  • A microSD card reader for the computer.

2. Stop updates

System Settings > System > Auto-Update Software: turn it off.

3. Move Fortnite to the microSD card

  1. Turn the Switch off completely (hold Power, Power Options > Turn Off), insert the card, turn it on.
  2. System Settings > Data Management > Manage Software, choose Fortnite.
  3. Move Data (or Move Data Between System / microSD Card), then Move to microSD Card.

4. Copy the card to a computer

  1. Turn the Switch off completely, then take the card out.
  2. Put the card in the computer and copy the card's whole Nintendo folder, keeping its structure, to a folder named after the version, for example switch-fortnite-12.41.
  3. Make a manifest: 
    retrora-archive manifest switch-fortnite-12.41 switch-fortnite-12.41.json
Copy back with the same kind of computer you copied with (Windows to Windows, Mac to Mac), so the files keep the attributes the Switch expects.

5. Put it back

Copy the Nintendo folder back to a microSD card (the same card or a new one), put it in the same Switch, and Fortnite shows in the menu. The files are encrypted to that console; another Switch cannot use them.

6. Common problems

  • The card is not recognised: accept the exFAT system update, or use a card of 32 GB or less.
  • "Corrupt data" after copying back: the folder structure changed, or it was copied with a different kind of computer. Copy the whole Nintendo folder again, exactly as it was.
