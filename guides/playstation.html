PlayStation (PS4 and PS5)

The PS5 can move games to a USB drive and back. The PS4 can back up the whole console, games included, to a USB drive. Both copies are encrypted to your console and account, and both are real archives of the version you had.

On this page
  1. PS5: USB Extended Storage
  2. PS4: Back Up and Restore
  3. Keeping it on a computer
  4. Common problems

1. PS5: USB Extended Storage

You need

  • A USB drive of 250 GB to 8 TB, SuperSpeed USB 5 Gbps or faster. Formatting erases it.

Steps

  1. Stop updates: Settings > Saved Data and Game/App Settings > Automatic Updates, turn off Auto-Download.
  2. Connect the drive to a rear USB port (or a SuperSpeed front port).
  3. Settings > Storage > USB Extended Storage > Format as USB Extended Storage.
  4. Settings > Storage > Console Storage > Games and Apps > Move Items, tick Fortnite, then Move.
  5. Label the drive with the version and date. To play, move it back the same way (PS5 games play from the console's own storage; PS4 games can play from the USB drive).

2. PS4: Back Up and Restore

You need

  • A USB drive formatted FAT32 or exFAT, with at least twice the space your installed games use.

Steps

  1. Stop updates: Settings > System > Automatic Downloads, turn off Application Update Files.
  2. Plug in the drive. Settings > System > Back Up and Restore > Back Up PS4.
  3. Make sure Applications is included, name the backup, and start it. The PS4 restarts and backs up.
  4. The backup is the PS4\EXPORT\BACKUP folder on the drive. Copy that folder to a computer as well.

Restoring

Settings > System > Back Up and Restore > Restore PS4. This erases the PS4 and puts back everything in the backup, Fortnite included. Sign in with the same account afterwards.

3. Keeping it on a computer

  • PS4 backup: copy the PS4 folder from the drive, then retrora-archive manifest PS4 ps4-backup.json.
  • PS5 extended storage: computers do not open this drive. Keep the drive itself, or make a whole-drive image exactly as in the Xbox guide's imaging step, and do not let Windows format it.

4. Common problems

  • The PS5 will not format the drive: it is smaller than 250 GB or slower than SuperSpeed 5 Gbps.
  • The PS4 says there is not enough space: use a larger drive. The backup needs room to spare.
  • Fortnite will not start from an old copy: its servers require the newest version. The copy is an archive.
